# Portfolio home (index.html)

Scope: the portfolio's single page. Visitor mode: Experience, with the work leading from the first viewport. Audience: hiring managers in Belgrade and on international remote AI teams; job: decide in a minute whether to email Luka. Proof: BANKA_2 (25 engineers, 2,165 backend and 1,254 frontend tests, Arbitro), four released Windows apps, Stem Agent, Nexus. Unresolved: none.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The portfolio is one brochure in a Unigrid-style identity program: a black title band over a strict modular grid, with BANKA_2 shown as a clean diagram of its real services. It refuses the developer-portfolio default of headline, buttons and a card grid.

OWN-WORLD: True black band, white paper, one colour per publication (the red request path here, each app's icon colour on its own site). Archivo grotesk set tight. 6px black rules over headings, one-pixel rules between rows; modules come from grid position, never cards or shadows. The diagram: flat square service blocks with a 3px top rule, the gateway as the one black block, right-angled wires, the request path in red.

STORY: The visitor reads who Luka is and which roles he wants in the band, sees the system he led in the diagram, scans dated experience and the shipped apps, and emails him.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Black band about 38% of a 900px viewport: small nav row; the name in two lines near 92px flush left; one-sentence sub; on the right the Open to roles block with Email me (white, primary) and GitHub. Directly below, the BANKA_2 diagram across the grid with its legend in the left four columns.

FORM: Unigrid, Vignelli's National Park Service brochure system; position 5 of the ordered list; seed key cc488279. Signature interaction: pointing at, focusing or tapping a service in the diagram lifts its block and shows what it does in the legend. Motion: the red request wires draw in once on load, then everything holds still.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
