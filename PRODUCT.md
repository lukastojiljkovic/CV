# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Hiring managers and recruiters deciding whether to interview Luka, weighted equally between international teams hiring remotely (AI/ML startups and labs) and engineering organisations in Belgrade. They arrive from a CV, LinkedIn or GitHub, usually on a laptop, and give the page a minute or two before deciding whether to reach out.

The four app websites (Refreshify, PermaDel, PerfWindow, Pwrschdlr) serve two visitors at once: Windows users deciding whether to download the app, and recruiters clicking through from the portfolio to judge the craft.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio for Luka D. Stojiljković, a final-year Computer Science student in Belgrade who is actively looking for a job. Success is a hiring manager emailing him for an interview. The app websites succeed when a stranger understands the app and downloads it, and when a recruiter leaves convinced the work is finished and professional.

## Positioning

He has already led and built at scale: as team lead of BANKA_2 he led a 25-person team on an enterprise banking platform, wrote the majority of the code, and shipped it with 2,165 backend and 1,254 frontend tests plus a self-hosted LLM assistant (Arbitro). Next to that sit four released, installable Windows apps with CI and their own websites, an applied-AI research agent (Stem Agent) and an offline-first desktop workspace (Nexus). The leading belief a visitor should form: he leads teams and builds complete systems at scale.

## Operating Context

- Roles sought, in order: Applied AI, full-stack, backend. Based in Belgrade, open to remote.
- Visitors compare the page with other candidates' portfolios; it also ships as a one-page PDF CV (CV_Luka_Stojiljkovic.pdf) linked from the site.
- Static site on GitHub Pages: index.html, css/styles.css, js/main.js, self-hosted fonts. Each app site is a static `site/` folder in its own repository, deployed by a Pages workflow.
- Light and dark themes, following the system setting until the visitor chooses.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Contact: email (primary), GitHub, LinkedIn, phone in the PDF.
- Nexus is open source under Apache 2.0 but its repository is still private; the site must not claim it is public.
- Each app keeps its existing name, icon and icon colour.

## Brand Commitments

- An "Open to roles" card next to the name, listing Applied AI, full-stack and backend in that order.
- All public material is in English (Nexus's own UI stays Serbian).
- The user's brief: professional and clean, but beautifully designed. Two earlier directions were rejected: a Tetris/wide-type hero (gimmicky) and a standard SaaS landing template (generic).

## Evidence on Hand

- Experience, education and project facts in CV_Luka_Stojiljkovic.tex and index.html.
- Real app screenshots in light and dark: img/*.webp; app icons: img/icons/*.png.
- Real numbers: 25 engineers led, 2,165 backend tests, 1,254 frontend tests; app versions Refreshify 1.1, Pwrschdlr 1.0, PermaDel 1.0, PerfWindow 0.10.
- No testimonials, user counts, download counts, employers' logos or press. Never invent them.

## Product Principles

1. Proof over adjectives: every claim on the page is something a visitor can open, download or count.
2. Scale first: the 25-person BANKA_2 team and its test suites lead, the shipped apps prove range.
3. Respect a minute of attention: what he does, what he has built and how to reach him are clear immediately.
4. One system across the portfolio and the four app sites.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast in both themes, keyboard navigation with visible focus, reduced motion respected.
